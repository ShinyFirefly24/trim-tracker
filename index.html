❓ Help
1. Getting started

Trim Tracker records your work, tracks what you are owed, and produces reports. Data stays on this device. Use the tabs along the bottom: Home (add entries), Payments, Reports, Harvests, and Settings.

2. Adding entries

On Home, Add Entry records each job by weight (grams), hours, or expense. Each entry goes to the active harvest (★); choosing another asks first and makes it active. Edit or delete an entry from the Unpaid Entries list.

B-Buds (beside the strain) counts a weight separately from the strain's normal weight. Reports list normal weights and B-Buds separately, each with a total, then a combined total. Room records where the work was done.

Turn either off in the ⋮ menu on Add Entry. This only hides it on the form; nothing already recorded is changed or deleted.

3. Harvests

A harvest groups related entries; one is active at a time. The Harvests tab shows each as a card with notes, strain inventories (entered in pounds, shown in pounds and grams), entries, and reports.

Marking a harvest complete is only a label; you can still edit it. Deleting a harvest moves its entries, notes, and inventories to a harvest you choose.

4. Payments

Log a Payment applies the amount to your unpaid work, oldest first, regardless of job type. The Payment Estimator shows what unpaid work is worth at the rates you enter, without recording anything.

The ⋮ menu on each adds a separate B-Buds rate and separate hourly rates per job.

5. Reports

The Reports tab offers Entries, Financial, and All Unpaid reports for today, a day, a date range, all time, or one harvest. Each harvest also has its own Reports page. Use Save as Image to keep a report; the camera button on a section saves only that section.

6. Manager mode

For supervising a crew. Switch it on under App mode in Settings. Add Entry then has a Who selector to log weights and hours for yourself or your crew, and Reports adds Crew Reports (full log, by person, by strain). Switching back to Personal mode deletes nothing.

7. Settings

Manage your strains, job types, and rooms (alphabetical unless rearranged), the order of sections, and the color scheme. Pound Weight sets grams per pound (default 454, range 400–500); changing it updates every pound figure, including past reports, but never your logged grams.

8. Backup and restore

Data is stored only on this device, so download a backup regularly. Restore from Backup replaces all current data. Clear All Data in the Danger Zone cannot be undone.

9. Common questions

Did hiding B-Buds or Room delete anything?

No. It only hides the option on the form.

Why did a pound figure change?

Check Pound Weight in Settings.

How do I move to another device?

Download a backup, then restore it on the new device.
